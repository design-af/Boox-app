Dictation

Set up (once)

Open this inside the AFS Custom Boox app on the tablet.
1 Allow the microphoneNeeded to hear the presenter.
2 Speech engineChecking…
3 Turn on the AFS Dictation keyboardIn the list, switch on “AFS Dictation”, then come back.
4 Switch to itTap into a text box first, or pick it here.

Use it in any app

  1. Open your notes app and tap into the text where the notes should go.
  2. Switch keyboard to AFS Dictation (keyboard switcher, or step 4 above).
  3. Tap ● Start. It keeps listening and typing until you tap ■ Stop. Words in progress show underlined; they lock in when the phrase ends.
  4. ABC goes back to your normal keyboard (long-press for the full list). Punctuation and New line tapped mid-phrase are added when the phrase ends.

The screen stays on while listening. Closing the keyboard stops it. Every phrase is also saved to the backup below, in case a note app loses text.
