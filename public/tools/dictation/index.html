Dictation

Floating button

Open this inside the AFS Custom Boox app on the tablet.

A small MIC bubble floats over every app and your normal keyboard keeps working. Tap it to open the panel, long-press to start or stop recording, drag to move it. Drag the panel’s top grip to move the panel; tap the grip to switch between small and large.

Set up (once)

1 Allow the microphoneNeeded to hear the presenter.
2 Speech engineChecking…
3 Display over other appsLets the bubble and panel float on top. Switch it on for AFS Custom Boox, then come back.
4 Push into text fields (recommended)Settings → Accessibility → AFS Dictation → On. It only acts when you tap Push. Without it, Push copies the text so you can paste it.
5 Claude API key (for Check and Key points)Below in Settings. Without it, Check still shows words the speech engine was unsure of.

How it flows

  1. Live — tap ● Start. The talk is transcribed into Live; nothing is typed into your notes.
  2. Prep ▸ — at the end of a talk. Recording carries on: the next talk starts a fresh Live recording.
  3. Prep — Check steps through words that may be misheard (tap a fix, Keep or Delete). Key points summarises the talk. Edit opens it here to type changes.
  4. Push — tap into your notes, then Push. It inserts the transcript (or the key points, when showing them) at the cursor.
  5. Library — every recording is kept here and in the panel.
